FAQ

Know what Roof Scout is — and isn't.

Is this a roof inspection?

No. Roof Scout uses available imagery and AI to estimate visible roof condition. It is a prospecting and prioritization tool, not a physical inspection. Verify conditions on site before quoting or making a technical claim.

Where do the roof images come from?

The app can use aerial imagery and, when aerial imagery is unavailable, street-level sources such as Mapillary or Google Street View when the required keys are configured.

Can I verify a lead before unlocking it?

Yes. The scan shows the available context and lets you review flagged properties. Unlocks are used to reveal the full lead workflow and sales-ready details.

Do I have to buy leads before I know what I'm getting?

Roof Scout is built around scanning an area first, then choosing which results are worth unlocking. That keeps the workflow focused on your territory rather than a generic shared lead marketplace.
