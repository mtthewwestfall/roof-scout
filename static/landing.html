FAQ

Straight answers

Where do the roof images come from?

Current satellite and aerial imagery, the same kind insurers and appraisers use. When aerial views are inconclusive, we pull street-level photos so you still see the property.

Are the leads really exclusive?

Yes. You pick the ZIP, our AI finds the damage, and the leads are yours. We don't resell the same list to other roofers — that's the whole point.

What if a "lead" is a vacant or abandoned house?

Our system flags derelict properties into a review flow instead of burning your unlock. You approve or reject each one — rejected unlocks get replaced free.

Do I need a credit card for the trial?

No. The trial is 1 ZIP scan and 5 lead unlocks, completely free, no card. Paid plans start only when you choose one.

How accurate is the AI grading?

It grades visible damage — missing shingles, wear patterns, aging — on a 1–5 scale with plain-language notes. It's a targeting tool to aim your crew's time, not a replacement for an on-roof inspection.
