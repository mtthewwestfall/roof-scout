FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Roof Scout a roof inspection?
No. Roof Scout is a prospecting and opportunity-identification tool. It uses available imagery and signals to help contractors identify properties that may deserve closer attention. A qualified professional should inspect the roof before making conclusions about its condition.
Do I need a credit card to try it?
No. Your first ZIP scan and 5 lead unlocks are free — no credit card required.
How does Roof Scout identify opportunities?
It analyzes available rooftop imagery and other available signals to prioritize properties for prospecting, ranking visible signs of wear so you know which properties are worth a closer look.
Does Roof Scout guarantee roofing jobs?
No. Roof Scout helps identify prospects. Results depend on the market, homeowner response, your sales process, property condition, and many other factors.
Can I use Roof Scout for my service area?
Yes. Enter any ZIP code to explore available opportunities in the areas you serve.
