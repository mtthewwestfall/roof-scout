FAQ

Straight answers

Where do the roof images come from?

Current satellite and aerial imagery — the same kind insurers and appraisers rely on. Where aerial views are inconclusive, street-level photos fill the gap.

Are the leads really exclusive?

Yes. You choose the ZIP, the AI finds the damage, the leads are yours. We never resell a list — exclusivity is the product.

What about vacant or abandoned houses?

Derelict properties are flagged into a review flow instead of consuming your unlocks. Approve or reject each one; rejections are replaced free.

Do I need a credit card to try it?

No. The trial — one ZIP scan, five unlocks — is free with no card. You only pay when you decide it's working.

How accurate is the grading?

It scores visible damage — missing shingles, wear, aging — 1 to 5, with plain notes. It's a targeting instrument for your crew's time, not a substitute for the on-roof inspection.
