The problem with roofing lead generation

Most roofing leads are expensive, shared, and cold

Roofing contractors know the math on traditional lead generation: marketplaces charge $40–$110 per lead — and sell that same lead to four or five competing roofers. By the time you call, the homeowner has already heard from everyone else. Door-knocking burns daylight. Referrals are great but unpredictable.

Roof Scout takes a different approach to finding roofing jobs. Instead of buying shared contact lists, you scan a ZIP code and let AI review every residential rooftop in the area from available aerial imagery. Roofs showing visible signs of wear — aging materials, patching, tarps, discoloration — are graded and ranked so you can prioritize your prospecting on the properties most worth a closer look.

Every lead comes with:

  • A 1–5 condition grade — worst-first ranking across the whole ZIP
  • The address and map pin — no guessing where to go
  • Visual evidence — the AI pinpoints what it flagged on the aerial image
  • Plain-language findings — what was observed and why it matters
  • Storm history — nearby NOAA hail and wind events for context

This is a prospecting tool, not a promise. Roof Scout finds roofs worth a closer look and gives you the evidence to open the conversation — closing the job is still your craft.

No. Roof Scout is a prospecting tool: it finds roofs showing visible signs of wear and gives you the evidence to prioritize outreach. Closing jobs still depends on your sales process. We never promise guaranteed leads, appointments, jobs, or revenue.

Is Roof Scout a certified roof inspection?
No. Roof Scout provides AI-observed prospecting signals from available imagery — visible signs of wear worth a closer look. It is not a certified inspection or an engineering-grade assessment. Always verify conditions on-site before quoting work.
