Not all roofing leads are created equal. The single biggest factor in whether a lead is worth buying? How many other contractors got the same lead.

What "shared" really means

When you buy a lead from HomeAdvisor, Angi, or Thumbtack, you're buying a name and phone number that's simultaneously sold to 3–5 other roofing companies. The homeowner gets 5 calls within the hour. Here's what happens next:

  • Speed wins, not quality. The first caller gets the appointment. Everyone else gets voicemail.
  • Price becomes the only differentiator. When 5 roofers bid the same job, the homeowner picks the cheapest — not the best.
  • Close rates crater. Industry data puts shared-lead close rates at 10–20%, and that's for contractors with fast response systems.

What "exclusive" really means

An exclusive lead goes to one contractor. Nobody else is calling. The homeowner isn't comparing five quotes — they're having a conversation with you. Close rates on exclusive leads typically run 20–40%.

❌ Shared lead

  • $40–$110 per lead
  • 4–5 competitors calling
  • 10–20% close rate
  • Price-shopping homeowner
  • ~$500/customer

✅ Exclusive lead

  • $1–$25 per lead
  • Zero competitors
  • 20–40% close rate
  • Conversation, not bidding war
  • ~$12–$60/customer

Where do exclusive leads come from?

Referrals are the classic source — but you can't scale them on demand. The newer approach: generate your own exclusives through prospecting. Canvassing does this (you found the homeowner, nobody else has the lead). AI-powered prospecting does it at scale — scanning aerial imagery to find roofs showing visible wear, so you're reaching out before the homeowner has talked to anyone.

The key insight: the best exclusive lead is one your competitors don't even know exists yet. A homeowner who hasn't requested quotes isn't price-shopping. They're listening.
